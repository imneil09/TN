State Government Announces Major IT Park Project in Agartala Smart City

The new project aims to create over 5,000 jobs for local youth and boost the digital economy of the Northeast.

By Neil Roy

Updated: Dec 27, 2025 • 2:30 PM

ADVERTISEMENT Leaderboard Ad

AGARTALA: In a significant move to boost the technological landscape of the state, the Tripura government today officially announced the construction of a state-of-the-art IT Park within the Smart City premises.

The project, valued at approximately ₹500 Crores, is expected to be completed by 2027. Speaking at the press conference, the Chief Minister highlighted the importance of retaining local talent.

AD Rectangle Ad

"Our youth have immense potential. With this IT hub, we aim to bring world-class opportunities to their doorstep," he stated. The park will host facilities for startups, a data center, and offices for multinational corporations.

Experts believe this move will attract significant investment from neighboring countries and strengthen the 'Act East' policy. Infrastructure development, including high-speed fiber internet and uninterrupted power supply, has already commenced in the designated zone.
